| A10 Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF)  | `validate_outbound_url()` rejects private/loopback/link-local/reserved IPs (literal or DNS-resolved), the cloud metadata address, and non-http(s) schemes — called when an outbound-webhook endpoint is saved **and again immediately before every delivery** | `backend/app/core/url_guard.py`, `backend/tests/test_url_guard.py`, `backend/tests/modules/test_webhooks.py`, `backend/tests/test_webhook_delivery_job.py` |

`url_guard.py` gained its first caller in T-320, as planned — the outbound-webhook module, which is the only place this product fetches a URL somebody else chose. The guard runs **twice**, and the second call is the load-bearing one: a hostname that resolved to a public address when the endpoint was saved can resolve to `169.254.169.254` by the time the delivery goes out, because DNS belongs to whoever registered the URL. A destination that fails the check at delivery time is dead-lettered immediately rather than retried — retrying is five more requests at an internal address.
